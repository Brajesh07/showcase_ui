## What
Free Spelling Bee word game: form words from a 7-letter honeycomb hive including the center letter, with daily and unlimited modes plus a solver.

## For
Casual word-game players who want daily puzzles, practice, rankings, and related word games on the same site.

## Note
Also offers Spelling Bee Solver, hints via short ad clips, and sister games (WordGuess, Connect, Wordly, etc.). Stack not stated on the page.
